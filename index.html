Executive Brief: REGULATOR is a zero-hardware Progressive Web App (PWA) built for motor clubs and Clerks of the Course. Utilizing smartphone GPS, it automatically enforces geofenced speed limits across Service Parks, noise zones, and recce stages while streaming targeted HQ communications and safety telemetry.

Core Capabilities
  • Geofenced Speed Governance: Auto-detects Service Parks, noise zones, or recce stage bounds `[lat, lng]` and sets visual speed limits instantly on the competitor dashboard.
  • Event-Driven Telemetry: Transmits data to HQ only on speed breaches or alert triggers, keeping bandwidth low and HQ focused on incidents.
  • Targeted Command Messaging: Issue individual notices to a specific car (e.g., "Car #1: Watch your speed") or 1-tap broadcasts to ALL CARS for emergency red flags.
  • Instant Crew SOS Controls: High-contrast OK, FIRE, and MEDICAL buttons log exact GPS coordinates formatted for emergency response.

Technical Specifications

Built on HTML5/JavaScript connected to a Firebase Firestore backend. Geofence tracking utilizes line-segment corridor calculations and Haversine distance formulas. Competitors simply open the web URL, enter their car number, and mount their phone on the dashboard.
